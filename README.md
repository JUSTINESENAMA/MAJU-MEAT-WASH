# *PROJECT OVERVIEW: MAJUS’S MEAT&WASH*

*1. Business Concept* 
Majus’s Meat&Wash is a 3-in-1 lifestyle hub based in *Rustenburg Mogwase, Zone 5 Malema Street*. We combine three high-demand kasi services under one roof:
- *Professional Car Wash*: Full-service wash for cars, vans, and taxis
- *Chesa Nyama*: Fresh meat braaied to order with pap, chakalaka, and sauce 
- *Hubbly Lounge*: Outdoor seating area with hubbly rentals for relaxation

Tagline: _“Where your car eats dirt, and you eat meat.”_

*2. Target Market*
- *Primary*: Local drivers, taxi operators, families, and groups of friends in Mogwase and greater Rustenburg
- *Secondary*: Weekend visitors, people attending local events, anyone wanting food + car care + social time in one stop
- *Customer Need*: Save time and money by combining errands with leisure

*3. Services & Pricing*

Service	Options	Price
**Car Wash**	Normal Car	R70
	Van	R90
	Taxi	R150
**Chesa Nyama**	Platter for 1	R70
	Platter for 2	R100
	Platter for 4	R300
**Hubbly Lounge**	Hubbly Rental	R200
	Refill	R100

*4. Operations Model* 
_How customers flow through the business:_
1. *Pull In*: No booking needed. Staff guides vehicle to wash bay
2. *Order & Relax*: Customer places food/hubbly order at counter, then sits in lounge area 
3. *We Handle The Rest*: Car gets washed while meat is braaied and hubbly is set up
4. *One Bill*: Customer pays for all services together before leaving

*5. Key Differentiators*
- *Time Efficiency*: Complete 3 activities in 30-45 minutes vs. 3+ hours separately
- *Quality*: Professional wash products + fresh meat braaied daily to order
- *Atmosphere*: Safe, social kasi vibe with music and proper seating
- *Convenience*: Open 7 days, Mon-Sun 9am till late. Cash & Card accepted
